On Setting Text for the Screen

A short essay on rhythm, measure and the quiet work that good type does when nobody is looking. Read it at your own pace; the argument is deliberately simple.

Why measure matters

Line length decides how far the eye travels before it returns to the left edge. Most readers are comfortable somewhere around 60 to 75 characters per line, which is why this column is capped near 760 pixels. The full specification lives in the type guidelines, and the earlier draft is archived here.

Vertical rhythm follows from the baseline. With a line height of 1.6, each line sits far enough from its neighbour to breathe, and a paragraph gap of one line keeps sections distinct without turning the page into a list of islands.
A forced line break, like this one, is useful for addresses and poems but should be rare in running text.

Scale and hierarchy

A modular scale gives headings a consistent relationship to body text. Here the steps are expressed in rem so that they follow the user's preferred base size.

Choosing a typeface

Serif faces suit long reading, while sans-serif faces are often preferred for interface labels and captions. Mixing the two is common, provided each has a clear job.

Practical tip

Test every heading at the smallest supported width before you ship it.

Note on sources

Inline emphasis and marks

Emphasis should be used sparingly. Highlighted passages help a reader find their place during revision, but they are not a substitute for structure. Water is H2O, and the area of a circle is πr2. Footnotes are often set in smaller type, typically around 13 pixels, so that they do not compete with the main text.

A quotation worth keeping

Typography exists to honour content. It is the craft of endowing human language with a durable visual form.

Robert Bringhurst, The Elements of Typographic Style

Code in running text

Inline identifiers such as line-height or max-width sit on the baseline. Longer examples belong in a block:

body {
  max-width: 760px;
  margin: 0 auto;
  line-height: 1.6;
  font-size: 1.0625rem;
}

Lists remain simple:

  • Set body text between 16 and 18 pixels.
  • Keep measure between 45 and 75 characters.
  • Use one typeface family per role.
