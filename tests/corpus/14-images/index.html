Morning on the lake

Photographed before the first light reached the shore.

Maren Holt

Landscape photographer and writer based in the north. Shoots mostly on film.

Working before dawn

The best light arrives in the few minutes before the sun clears the ridge. Planning the walk in the dark is most of the work.

Each frame here was exposed for four seconds on a tripod, with the shutter released by a cable.
